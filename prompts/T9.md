`shop.db` is a SQLite database (tables: customers, orders, order_items, refunds; inspect it yourself). Write three SQL files that each run with `sqlite3 shop.db < file.sql` and print CSV-free plain rows (default sqlite3 output, `|` separated, no headers):
1. `q1.sql`: net revenue in pounds per country for PAID orders only, where net = sum(qty*unit_price_pence) minus all refunds for those orders, in pounds rounded to 2 decimals. Customers with no country count as 'Unknown'. Order by net revenue descending. Columns: country|net_pounds.
2. `q2.sql`: for each month of 2026 that has at least one paid order (format YYYY-MM), the number of distinct customers who placed their FIRST ever paid order in that month. Order by month. Columns: month|new_customers.
3. `q3.sql`: the top 3 customers by total paid gross spend (before refunds), with ties broken by customer name ascending. Columns: name|gross_pounds (2 decimals).
Be careful: an order can have several items and several refund rows. Do not ask questions; check your results, then finish.
