# Shoot Plan: London & Manchester (2 days)

## Weather (from get_weather tool)
| City | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| London | Light rain, 12°C, wind 18 km/h | Cloudy, 14°C, wind 10 km/h |
| Manchester | Heavy rain, 10°C, wind 25 km/h | Sunny intervals, 13°C, wind 12 km/h |

## Suggested schedule
- **Day 1: London** – light rain, so the shoot is feasible with protection. Manchester's heavy rain and 25 km/h wind make it the worse choice for Day 1.
- **Day 2: Manchester** – sunny intervals and light wind, better for exterior shots. Travel London → Manchester the evening of Day 1.
- Keep a covered or indoor backup for London Day 1 in case the rain gets heavier.

## Checklist (docs/shoot_checklist.md) applied
- [ ] Rain covers for camera + mic. **Mandatory on Day 1 (London rain)**. Pack for Manchester as well in case the forecast shifts.
- [ ] 2 spare batteries per camera. Cold and wet conditions drain batteries faster.
- [ ] Release forms for anyone on camera.
- [ ] Wind muff on mics. **Needed on London Day 1 (18 km/h)**. Not needed in the Day 2 Manchester forecast (12 km/h), but bring it anyway.
- [ ] Call sheet sent 24h before each shoot day. London's goes out the day before Day 1, and Manchester's by Day 1 morning.
