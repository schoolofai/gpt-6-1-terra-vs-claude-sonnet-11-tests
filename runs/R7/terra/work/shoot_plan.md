# Two-day outdoor shoot plan

**Schedule:** Day 1 in London; Day 2 in Manchester. Dates are not yet set. The weather tool labels its forecast only as Day 1 and Day 2, without dates or issue time; confirm the shoot dates align with those labels and recheck before committing.

## Weather and city choice

| City | Day 1 | Day 2 |
|---|---|---|
| London | Light rain, 12°C, wind 18 km/h | Cloudy, 14°C, wind 10 km/h |
| Manchester | Heavy rain, 10°C, wind 25 km/h | Sunny intervals, 13°C, wind 12 km/h |

Source: `node tools/get_weather.js London` and `node tools/get_weather.js Manchester`. Shoot London on Day 1 and Manchester on Day 2 to avoid Manchester's forecast heavy rain on Day 1.

## Day 1 — London

- **09:00–10:00:** Crew call, location and safety check, fit camera and mic rain covers and mic wind muffs.
- **10:00–12:30:** Shoot priority outdoor scenes; monitor rain and protect equipment.
- **12:30–13:30:** Lunch and equipment check.
- **13:30–16:30:** Shoot remaining scenes and pickups; pause or move to a prearranged sheltered location if conditions become unsafe.
- **16:30–17:00:** Wrap, back up footage and pack. Travel to Manchester that evening; book transport and accommodation in advance and verify timings.

## Day 2 — Manchester

- **09:00–10:00:** Crew call, location and safety check, prepare equipment.
- **10:00–12:30:** Shoot priority outdoor scenes during the forecast sunny intervals.
- **12:30–13:30:** Lunch and equipment check.
- **13:30–16:30:** Remaining scenes and pickups; use shelter or reschedule if weather becomes unsafe.
- **16:30–17:00:** Wrap, back up footage and check equipment.

Times are provisional and subject to location access, permissions, travel and confirmed crew availability.

## Shoot checklist

From `docs/shoot_checklist.md` (found with `node tools/search_docs.js 'shoot checklist'`):

- [ ] Pack rain covers for camera and mic; mandatory for London's forecast rain. Carry them to Manchester as a contingency.
- [ ] Pack two spare batteries per camera.
- [ ] Obtain release forms for everyone on camera.
- [ ] Fit wind muffs on mics when wind is over 15 km/h: required for London's Day 1 forecast of 18 km/h. Bring them for Manchester too.
- [ ] Send the call sheet 24 hours before the shoot.
- [ ] Confirm locations, any required permissions, crew, travel and backup shelter; recheck weather before each day.
